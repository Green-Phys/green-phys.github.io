Our new version paper, **[Green/WeakCoupling 1.0.0: Symmetry-adapted self-consistent many-body perturbation theory for molecules and solids](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2026.110486)**, is now published in *Computer Physics Communications*.

<!--more-->

The paper presents the symmetry-adapted Green/WeakCoupling 1.0.0 release for self-consistent many-body calculations of molecules and solids.

**Authors:** Gaurav Harsha, Agnieszka Jażdżewska, Antoine Marie, Munkhorgil Wang, Sergei Iskakov, Dominika Zgid, and Emanuel Gull.

*Computer Physics Communications*, 110486 (2026). DOI: [10.1016/j.cpc.2026.110486](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2026.110486).

See the [GREEN 1.0 release announcement](/news/v1.0.0/) for an overview of the new capabilities and links to the software and benchmarks.
